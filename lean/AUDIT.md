## Mathematical premises

The numerical bound and first-coefficient equality condition in Theorem 1.2, and the disk bounds of Theorem 1.3, use normalized disk Loewner existence. The full slit-map equality characterization in Theorem 1.2 also assumes the classical exterior area-sum estimate for the map.
